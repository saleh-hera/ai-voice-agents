# Demo Video Script — AI Digital Dealer Walkthrough

Purpose: a screen-recorded walkthrough of the build-sheet artifact
(https://claude.ai/code/artifact/ffc3e19f-deac-4eda-a5fe-1589aa3257d7), for
use in cold emails, the website, and pitch calls. Target length: 60-90 seconds.
Record with any free screen recorder (OBS, Loom, Windows Game Bar) — no
special tooling needed, just narrate over the artifact while clicking through it.

## Before recording
- Open the artifact link, resize the browser to a clean width (no dev tools,
  no extra tabs visible)
- Have the business info on Step 1 already thought through — script below
  assumes you keep the default "Bright Smile Dental" example, but swap in a
  real prospect's business name if recording a personalized version for them

## Shot-by-shot script

**[0:00–0:10] — Step 1: Business**
*(Screen: Step 1 form, cursor in the Business Name field)*

> "This is what it looks like when a business signs up for an AI Digital Dealer.
> First — their business info. Name, type, city, what they actually sell."

*(Action: click into a field or two, don't over-explain each one)*

**[0:10–0:30] — Step 2: Choose Modules**
*(Screen: click "Continue to modules", land on the module picker)*

> "Then they pick what they actually need — not a one-size-fits-all bot.
> Missed-call recovery, appointment reminders, review requests, even
> marketing modules like local SEO or social content."

*(Action: toggle 2-3 modules on, let the price update visibly)*

> "The price updates live as they build exactly the system their business needs."

*(Action: point at or hover the running total / tier pill)*

**[0:30–0:55] — Step 3: See It In Action**
*(Screen: click "See it in action")*

> "And here's the part that matters — what it actually sounds like."

*(Action: scroll through the call transcript slowly, pause on 1-2 key lines)*

> "The AI answers, asks the right questions, and books the appointment —
> right there in the conversation. Then if they enable it, it automatically
> texts back missed calls, sends reminders, and asks for reviews afterward.
> No one on the team has to remember to do any of it."

**[0:55–1:10] — Step 4: The offer**
*(Screen: scroll to the trial CTA card)*

> "Whatever they build, it starts with a 7-day free trial — no setup fee,
> no commitment. If it doesn't save them time or bring in more customers,
> they don't keep it."

**[1:10–1:15] — Close**
*(Screen: pull back to show the full page, or cut to a plain end card if you add one)*

> "That's an AI Digital Dealer — one system, built to fit any business, from a
> single receptionist to the whole customer funnel. Reply and I'll set one up
> for your business specifically."

## Notes for cutting it down further (30-sec version)
If you need something tighter for a cold email attachment, cut straight from
the 0:10-0:30 module-toggle beat into the 0:30-0:55 transcript beat, and drop
the Step 1 business-form section entirely — the module picker + live
transcript is the most convincing part on its own.

## Where to use it
- Attach or link in the v4 "Total Solution" Email 2 follow-up
  (sales/email-templates.md) as "see what it sounds like" instead of just
  text
- Website: could replace/augment the existing static demo section once
  reviewed
- Do NOT publish this as a public artifact URL in outreach without deciding
  first whether you want prospects able to freely reconfigure your pricing
  live — consider whether a locked/read-only version is worth building before
  wide distribution.
