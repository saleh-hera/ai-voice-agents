## Reply snippets for common questions

**"How is this different from Synthflow / Vapi directly?"**
> Vapi is the infrastructure — amazing, but you still have to design prompts, voices, and call flows yourself. AI Agents Vault is the "done for you" layer: industry-specific agents already built, tested, and tuned for real calls. Think Shopify themes vs. writing your own storefront.

**"Does it work with my existing phone number?"**
> Yes — we set up call forwarding from your existing line, or provision a dedicated number. Live within 24 hours either way.

**"What about HIPAA / compliance?"**
> MediAssist (healthcare) and DentaDesk are built with HIPAA-compliant handling; LexAssist follows legal-intake confidentiality practices. Happy to go deeper on specifics.

**"Pricing?"**
> $179–$399/month depending on the agent, 7-day free trial, no setup fees, cancel anytime. A human receptionist runs $2,500+/month — that's the comparison that matters.
